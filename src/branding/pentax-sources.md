# PENTAX official source artwork

Retrieved from Ricoh's official sites on 2026-11-10. Files are original downloads; no glyphs have been redrawn.

Preferred vector source: `pentax-k3iii-official.svg`

- Asset: https://www.ricoh-imaging.co.jp/english/products/k-3-3/top/img/logo-pentax-k3iii.svg
- Official product page: https://www.ricoh-imaging.co.jp/english/products/k-3-3/
- Full SVG viewBox: `0 0 377.83 178.62`.
- The **first path**, `class="a"`, contains only the complete PENTAX wordmark. It occupies approximately x=55.72..326.64 and y=0..52.68. Remaining paths are the camera model text.
- The original path is black. Reusing its unchanged geometry with a red fill is an adaptation for the requested cover, not an unmodified official red asset.

Independent transparent raster alternative: `pentax-vision-mobile-original.png`

- Asset: https://www.ricoh-imaging.co.jp/english/brand/pentax/vision/img/logo_sp.png
- Official page: https://www.ricoh-imaging.co.jp/english/brand/pentax/vision/
- RGBA, 233 × 46 pixels, white letters on transparency. Desktop variant `pentax-vision-original.png` is 123 × 24 pixels.

Additional exact official shape reference: `pentax-og-original.png`

- Asset: https://www.ricoh-imaging.co.jp/common/img/og-image-pentax.png
- Referenced by the official film-project page: https://www.ricoh-imaging.co.jp/english/pentax/filmproject/
- 1200 × 630 pixels overall, dark wordmark on white; the actual central mark is approximately 412 × 80 pixels.

Other downloads in this directory (`pentax-brand-original.jpg`, `pentax-europe-original.jpg`, and `pentax-footer-original.png`) are reference candidates, not the recommended cover artwork.
